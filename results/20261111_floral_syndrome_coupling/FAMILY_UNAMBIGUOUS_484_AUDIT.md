# Family-disjoint floral trait prediction: strict unambiguous taxonomy and equal-family uncertainty

**11 November 2026 · empirical original-input independent-code audit, fd PR #14 · exploratory.** This revisits the positive family-heldout GloBI documentation-composition result and resolves an important difference between a *genus-weighted family-cluster bootstrap* and an *equally weighted botanical-family bootstrap*.

## Why this new pass is necessary

The [earlier family-disjoint analysis](FAMILY_DISJOINT_GLOBI_TRANSFER.md) scored 486 matched botanical genera and 88 families, assigning a provisional majority family to two genus names with conflicting frozen GBIF family annotations. Its first-family-partition ridge-100 improvement from three coarse Direct floral morphology features was +0.001519 five-channel MSE (3.62% reduction), with a conditional genus-weighted family-cluster bootstrap interval entirely above zero.

We independently retrieved the exact frozen `island` GBIF taxon snapshot at commit `3bfa818ffbc7a0dea4319933c4b0a0eb41cfabb5`, blob `5ae92313517118f0dbb29d8863aa2e379ce88747`, and linked it to the **same original protected island Direct species trait ZIP** (SHA256 `b7d7f357d9d4d93062abc4671f27434725f6e0da07c48c1c574c2b601c64f999`) and **same GloBI source-genus v2 ZIP** (SHA256 `475dda8b0eef4ce22ad1aa736fa82cc049042d801217c65d52641489288ca254`).

In the source GBIF genus-to-family table all 486 matched genera have at least one label, but **two have competing family labels**. Rather than choosing a majority family for a family-disjoint test, the strict sensitivity discards both entirely. This yields **484 genera, 1,590 directly recorded strict single-state morphological species, 88 uniquely assigned families**. No genus-to-family identities, raw taxa or GloBI provider records are published.

## Matched-population prediction: genus versus whole-family withholding

All models have identical 484 genus test units, share the same GloBI five-channel *documented reference share* endpoint, the same source-evidence controls (log independent GloBI references; log documented plant names; log Direct morphology names), training-only standardization, fixed ridge alpha100, and simplex-projected nonnegative fractions summing to 1.

| Model | Whole GENUS 5-fold MSE | Whole FAMILY GroupKFold 5-fold MSE | Family-GroupKFold gain over source-evidence only |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Source evidence only | 0.041493 | 0.041756 | baseline |
| + 3 coarse morphology proportions | **0.039953** | **0.040708** | **+0.001048 (2.51%)** |
| + 22 individual category marginal proportions | 0.037876 | 0.041707 | **+0.000049 (effect nearly absent)** |
| + full high-dimensional colour×form×symmetry joint profiles | 0.044393 | 0.052824 | **−0.011068 (worse)** |

These are the **unambiguous-family 484-genus subset** and do not silently replace or contradict the earlier 486-genus family-balanced main analysis. Differences in family assignments, test set and exact fold construction change numerical MSE. Five additional outcome-blind **balanced family** partitions and two fixed ridge strengths both retain positive gains for the coarse-three-feature model: alpha100 range +0.001048–+0.001422 including fixed GroupKFold; alpha10 range +0.000904–+0.001356. Detailed joint models worsen in all these variants.

### The uncertainty estimator matters

The large-family-weighted estimate is not the same as a **one-family-one-vote** estimator:
- The 484-genus family-GroupKFold coarse model has **genus-equal gain +0.001048**.
- The **family-equal average** gain is **+0.001074**, but just **48 of 88** family means are positive.
- Family-equal resampling (4,999 draws over 88 family means) gives an exploratory conditional 95% interval **[−0.000226,+0.002346]**, which **includes zero**.
- The earlier PR report's positive interval [+0.000403,+0.002716] used a **genus-weighted family-cluster** resample in a different 486-genus cohort, so it cannot establish a universally positive, equally-weighted family effect.

To prevent this misinterpretation, public `src/fd/globi_family_holdout_cv.py` now reports **both distinct family-weighted and genus-weighted bootstrap intervals**, and explicitly rejects `AMBIG:` taxonomy labels. Existing fields remain for backward compatibility, with the new `family_equal_bootstrap_lower/upper` named distinctly. Synthetic tests verify strict family-state rejection and both uncertainty types.

### Not driven by only the largest families

The two biggest families in the 484-genus candidate dataset contain 58 and 49 genera (107/484); the five largest contain 36.0% of genera. Excluding those two from **heldout test scoring only**, without retraining, changes the observed fixed-family-GroupKFold coarse MSE gain to **+0.001464** on the remaining 377 genera; excluding the five largest leaves **+0.000963** on 310 genera. Positive sign is therefore not exclusively generated by two dominant families, but individual family effects remain heterogeneous.

### Family-preserving three-trait placebo

For the frozen GroupKFold-family predictions (484 genera, alpha100), permute the **whole three-dimensional morphology predictor vector** across genera **within the same family**; do not shuffle the GloBI recorded channel target or source-reference effort. There are 51 multi-genus families and **447** shufflable genera. Over **999** permutations:
- Observed pooled prediction gain = **+0.001048**;
- Null median = **+0.000259**, null 95% interval **[−0.000124,+0.000695]**;
- Posthoc upper-tail fraction = **0.001**.

This discounts the rival explanation that the signal is **only** the *average morphology* of each family. It does **not** remove shared traits within subfamilies/clades, source publication selection, genotype correlations or GloBI channel documentation bias. A permutation tail fraction is not an independently preregistered ecological effect.

## Which floral proxy actually carries the small cross-family information?

On the same strict 484-genus subset, recompare three coarse morphology axes across five independent balanced family partitions at alpha100. Average MSE gains over source-documentation controls:

| Added Direct morphological proxy | Average five-family-split MSE gain |
| --- | ---: |
| Inconspicuous green/brown colour alone | **−0.000017** |
| Actinomorphic symmetry alone | +0.000501 |
| Open-radial form alone | +0.001065 |
| **Actinomorphic symmetry + open-radial form** | **+0.001349** |
| All three including green/brown inconspicuous colour | +0.001331 |

The colour term adds an average **−0.000019** after the two structural proxies. The two-structural-feature model's GroupKFold observed gain is +0.001081, and 999 within-family **intact two-feature** placebo shuffles give null median +0.000336, null 95% interval [+0.000016,+0.000697], posthoc upper-tail 0.001. The structural gain's **family-equal bootstrap** interval also crosses zero ([−0.000294,+0.002310]).

Thus the **coarse structural form/accessibility morphology**, rather than this particular coarse colour state or full 115-feature trait combination, carries the most stable predictive *GloBI documentation-share* signal.

## Scientific conclusion and stop rule

**Supported, carefully framed:** across previously unseen botanical families, simple directly recorded floral structural traits convey a *small and heterogeneously distributed* amount of information about the global distribution of **DOCUMENTED** GloBI flower-interaction reference types, conditional on record-count controls. The pattern persists under multiple family fold assignments and family-preserving genus-level morphology shuffles.

**Not established:** a family-independent universal effect under equal-family uncertainty, actual flower–pollinator morphological fit, field visitation or effective pollen deposition, local bee/butterfly/moth/bird presence, island pollinator replacement, convergent syndrome evolution or causal selection. The source GloBI Lepidoptera channel does not separate butterflies from moths. Family names come from a frozen candidate GBIF occurrence checklist, not an independently verified full phylogeny.

**Priority for a stronger ecological paper:** calibrate the GloBI records to independent local visit counts/effectiveness or provider-heldout documentation, improve family/source lineage standardisation, and treat floral architecture as a narrow predictor, not as a proven named pollination syndrome. The separate 928-island morphology pairing result and its local five-channel detection gate remain conceptually distinct.
