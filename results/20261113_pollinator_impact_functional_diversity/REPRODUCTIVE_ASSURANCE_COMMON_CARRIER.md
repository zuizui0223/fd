## Why a new independent axis was needed

The 3-trait Direct floral morphology records measure *flower-colour × form × symmetry* categories, not actual reproduction or visit-based fitness. Reproductive assurance has biologically separate axes. We therefore analyze only Direct high/medium, strictly single-state source evidence:
- self compatibility `self_incompatibility = SC` (against SI);
- mating-system selfing `predominantly_selfing/obligate_selfing` (against predominantly outcrossing);
- autonomous/delayed selfing `autonomous_selfing_capacity = autonomous/delayed` (against absent).

All source Direct evidence has **6,726** plant species with strict direct SC/SI, **2,109** with strict mating-system selfing/outcrossing, **1,461** with strict autonomous/absent status (global source trait counts, not the fully floral-trait-complete species subset). Unknown/mixed/genus-inferred states are not zero or measured absence. Each island and potential five-nearest GIFT mainland source flora is thinned to **the same 20 independently trait-known plant carriers** in each of 20 replicates. Floral joint Rao Q is computed on the **identical reproductive-trait-known carriers**; it is not a separate 30-trait-complete group.

## Exact same island native-status sensitivity

A further within-island comparison asks whether changing **which recorded plants have strict native provenance** can account for differences (not an introduced-vs-native experiment):
- **67 northern-midlatitude islands with >=20 strict-native SC Direct carriers**: native-minus-all SC share **+0.06213 [+0.03719,+0.09331]** under whole-block resampling; the same islands' median source-relative SC difference shifts from **−0.025 all** to **+0.030 native**.
- **49 islands with >=20 native mating-system Direct carriers**: native-minus-all selfing share **+0.08499 [+0.05790,+0.11391]**; same-island median source-relative selfing shifts from **+0.0875 all** to **+0.175 native**.

Native-only is nested in all-recorded, and non-native vs unresolved provenance is not fully distinguished among the other taxa. The native subset may be geographically, taxonomically and literature-coverage selected. No plant-colonization or human introduction effect can be identified from this comparison alone.

## What is supported and what is NOT

**Supported descriptive macroecology:** recorded flower structural diversity and reproductive-assurance trait composition are separate axes of island floristic assembly. Compared with five nearest potential mainland-source floras, the source-relative SC and mating-system selfing percentages differ even on the **same directly trait-characterized plant carriers**, especially in well-supported northern-midlatitude floras. Native-provenance selection materially changes absolute directions and indicates why using all taxa as equivalent evidence is dangerous.

**Not supported mechanistically:** an actual pollinator decrease, functional guild switch, pollinator limitation relief by selfing, genetic adaptation, phenotypic plasticity, local fitness compensation or a universal selfing syndrome. The original 928 island potential pollinator guild evidence has no qualified within-region 5×4 local retained/adequately nondetected contrasts (0/20 pass). The original independent year-dataset temporal pilot has **only 3/80 islands** with repeated comparable GBIF data, not sufficient to identify decline. The supporting GloPL all-site +distance result and source-relative reproductive differences are separate observational endpoints, not a mediation chain.
