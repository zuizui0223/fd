## The actual new estimand: botanical genus composition, not species-level flower phenotype yet

The [previous genus-oracle ablation](../20261119_global_oracle_free_genus_assembly/README.md) found that the seven original strong geographically held-out trait transfers depended on knowing the TRUE target island genus composition: the simple other-island regional genus-source-weight prior improved only **one** Direct trait endpoint across three regions versus an outside-region genus-blind baseline and **none** versus the target-region Direct frequency baseline.

The next analysis therefore tests whether REAL independently documented island geography helps predict the **species-frequency-weighted botanical GENUS composition** of a withheld GIFT flora. The original study population is the **entire 4,505** accepted botanical island floras, not the historical 928 complete-trait subset and not only islands with directly scored phenotypes. This is a **descriptive floristic checklist composition** outcome; a genus not listed in a GIFT checklist is NOT an observed biological absence, dispersal failure, colonization event or ecological establishment probability.

The original GSHHG source geography includes **4,484** complete study islands with four climate PCs, area, distance to continent, valid coordinates, original 10-degree block and first GIFT geo_k5 candidate mainland entity. Every target is evaluated only using OTHER SOURCE GIFT island floras of the SAME independently mapped broad GSHHG region that occupy a DIFFERENT original 10-degree physical block and have a DIFFERENT first-ranked original candidate mainland source group. At least 30 donor floras across at least 10 different blocks and 10 different first-source groups are required; target needs ≥10 accepted species in ≥5 accepted genera. Every GIFT source whole-flora taxon name/genus stays protected.

The five source models all use exactly the same admissible original donor islands and category support: **region-equal source genus frequency baseline**, **40 geographically closest other-island floras**, **40 climate-nearest**, **40 climate+area+isolation-nearest**, and **40 environmental+ranked candidate-source entity Jaccard-nearest**. Original climate PCs and log1p(area) and log1p(continent distance) are robustly scaled on geography alone; nearest source island genus frequencies are weighted by original environmental/geographic distance and shrunk 20% toward the same region-equal reference. No actual target GIFT island genus roster or accepted species are used for the predictive distributions. Source genus unseen among all eligible donors is pooled into a COMMON unknown-genus output class (fixed 0.02), to avoid a target-genus oracle; this reduces resolution for genuinely novel genera and is shared by all predictive models.

## Original full GIFT botanical source results (all recorded native/introduced/unresolved taxa)

Prediction improvement is **region-equal genus-source Brier loss MINUS geographic/environmental predictor Brier loss** on the **same GIFT island accepted plant species**. Positive means the model predicts the whole-flora distribution of accepted genus-associated species labels better. The table shows original 10-degree GSHHG physical-block-equal means; each direction labelled supported was also checked by proper log loss and by separately original first candidate mainland-source-group-equal 399-bootstrap 95% intervals.

| Original broad GIFT source regime | GIFT floras scored | 40 geographically closest | Climate+area+isolation | Environment+ranked candidate-source similarity |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Northern midlatitude** | **1,773** | **+0.002688**, supported | **+0.001873**, supported | **+0.002264**, supported |
| **Tropical** | **992** | **+0.000461**, supported | +0.000134, uncertain | **+0.000256**, supported |
| **Southern extratropical** | **204** | +0.000936, uncertain | **+0.001286**, supported | **+0.001634**, supported |
| **Northern high latitude** | **270** | +0.002507, uncertain | +0.000854, uncertain | +0.001149, uncertain |

All reported source scores are **small absolute multiclass Brier-loss improvements** on a large sparse accepted-genus outcome vocabulary. This is not a percentage point increase of floral trait, not a measured selection strength or an island colonization rate. For the 3 primary macroregions, the combined *environment and ranked-source similarity* model is positively better than the simple region-equal donor genus frequency prior under BOTH proper Brier/log losses and BOTH geographically distinct uncertainty clusterings. It is **NOT** uniformly the best: northern midlatitude geographical proximity alone has a larger numerical Brier improvement than the environmental+source model, and its climate-only model also beats the region-equal baseline. In the tropics the climate+area/isolation model alone is ambiguous; first-source geography may be informative or may be correlated with other island spatial/plant sampling properties.

**Direct ecological source provenance falsification:** each model's being better than region-equal does NOT demonstrate that candidate mainland-source Jaccard provides extra prediction beyond climate+area+isolation, or that climate+area+isolation adds information beyond geographical proximity. Those **paired differences** require same-island source-score deltas and same-draw spatial/source-group resampling intervals; they have not been claimed from separate grouped means/intervals. The original paired-contrast follow-up [Actions #44482429856](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/actions/runs/44482429856) is a separate runner whose result must be checked before claiming that new incremental effect.

## Data availability and known-native restriction

Original GIFT parent **4,505**: source-complete physical/environment/first-source geographic polygons **4,484**; eligible for at least 10 accepted botanical species in five accepted genera are fewer, yielding all-recorded evaluated GIFT floras **1,773 north, 992 tropics, 204 southern extratropical, 270 northern high latitude**. All nonqualified source islands remain present in source-population accounting; no biological nonestablishment is inferred from missing geography/checklist resolution. Mean held-out focal accepted species belonging to genera NEVER seen among valid source donors (aggregated unknown-genus category) is **0.55% north, 1.08% tropics, 4.78% southern extratropical, 1.11% northern high latitudes**, demonstrating heterogeneity of source genus novelty.

For original **literal known-native** checklist entries, independent source-support is far smaller: **229 northern-midlatitude** and **131 tropical** source-scored floras, but **ZERO** southern extratropical and northern high-latitude floras with >=30 eligible distinct donor floras/independent source group support. Northern native environmental+source genus prediction is better than regional prior on both proper scores and both group interval checks, tropical native is uncertain; do NOT claim globally replicated native-only genus establishment prediction.

## How to interpret the macroecological result

The analysis supplies a REAL, full-original-frame check that local floristic genus compositions are not merely globally exchangeable accepted genus mixtures: original source spatial and environmental similarities can add conditional predictive information to taxonomic composition, even when the donor islands share neither the focal original physical geographic block nor the first candidate mainland-source entity.

**Not established:** true prospective biological genus arrival/colonization probabilities or dispersal kernels (the response is recorded genus composition), independent mainland ancestor floras (training comes from OTHER GIFT island floras), causal effects of island size, climate and isolation (confounded geographic correlates), source animal guild interactions/effective pollen delivery, within-island local floral phenotype adaptation, independent radiations or above-null convergence. Flora checklist incompleteness, original source incidence observation effort, genus-independent donor networks and complete original mainland pool coverage remain open inferential limits. The 399 group resampling intervals are exploratory and not multiplicity-adjusted.

**Main manuscript next step:** use the **geography-only-predicted taxonomic GENUS composition**, still without seeing the target's true botanical genera, as input weights in the previously source-verified **other-region same-genus plant floral category models**, and compare the resulting full island floral repertoire predictions with source regional trait-frequency genus-blind and previous focal genus-oracle predictions. Until completed, do NOT say the new source geography model restores the previous 7/13 cross-region floral trait forecast.
