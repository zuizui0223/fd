## Geographically plausible real source vs evidence-matched alternative assignment

The source comparator retains each focal test island's known original genera and tests proper categorical full joint colour/form probability prediction; **known genus is a conditional oracle**, not a prospective colonization predictor. The model uses a fixed category prior, genus direct-donor distribution shrinkage and a non-double-counting geographic-source genus composition correction. Evaluation uses exactly the SAME focal Direct plant species categories under the actual assigned five nearest GIFT native candidate regions and the two alternative source assignments.

For each focal island choose **two outcome-blind placebo source assignments** from other islands in the SAME broad northern-midlatitude geographical regime but DIFFERENT physical 10° block, with different original candidate source-entity sets and source identity-set Jaccard <0.8. Match the fraction of the focal directly trait-recorded plant genus carriers with a potential independent source donor congener to ±2 percentage points, and the source candidate distinct donor-species pool size within **0.5–2×** of actual. Candidate choice is sorted by support similarity and never conditioned on colour/form traits or a better score. A ±5 percentage point matching support is separately reported as a weaker, nested post-hoc sensitivity.

The score is **mean over original candidate island** of difference `pseudo-source loss − actual-source loss`, positive for actual closest-source advantage. A difference in proper categorical Brier and negative-log-likelihood is **a documentary geographic opportunity association**, not a causal selection mechanism, trait change, successful pollen service, or evolutionary independent replication.

## Current inference decision

**Conditional positive source prediction:** The original nearby GIFT assignment has a small descriptive additional predictive probability signal for previously untested species names in a highly selected, 215-island **n≥10 and ≥10% complete two-trait opportunity**, even after holding GIFT donor genus availability and distinct donor pool size approximately constant. But its proper logarithmic score geographic block uncertainty still overlaps zero, the same native/trait names are source-population dependent, and original donor morphology comes from a globally species-fixed island-sourced ledger. We therefore **do not identify** true historical colonization source, geological-origin floral selection, within-species adaptive evolution, realized pollinator replacement, reduced pollen limitation or reproductive fitness.
