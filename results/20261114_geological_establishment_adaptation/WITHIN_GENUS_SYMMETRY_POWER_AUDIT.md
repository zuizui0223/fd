## Tropical oceanic result and correct reading

The initial unconditioned same-island comparison requires at least three recorded endemic plants and three nonendemic native plants each with accepted distinct GIFT mainland candidate same-genus flower-symmetry evidence: **15 islands, 13 geographic blocks, 11 candidate first source groups**. Mean endemic minus native-nonendemic categorical symmetry discordance is **−0.0155809942**. Block-bootstrap conditional intervals were **[−0.0235518,−0.0085224]** and **[−0.0236077,−0.0089037]** in the two original numerical seeds. This is not a pre-registered multiple-test-adjusted result, and status group genus composition is not held constant.

On each same physical island, recompare ONLY endemic and native-nonendemic directly typed plants belonging to **identical plant genera**, with the same independent GIFT source congener genus reference. Eligible: **12 islands / 9 geographic blocks / 7 first-source groups**, totaling **57 shared genus × island units**. The source-relative endemic minus native symmetry difference is exactly **0.000000**, both bootstrap intervals **[0,0]**.

**Why this is NOT an informative negative evolutionary result:** Original categorical floral symmetry is *identical between endemic and native-nonendemic plants within all 57 available shared genus × island units*. The number of within-genus status groups with different observed symmetry is **0/57**, and groups with nonzero original source-congener discordance contrast are also **0/57**. The original pairing had zero within-genus variation available to test a departure. The matched point/interval zero is structurally forced. It CANNOT show that oceanic island floral evolution is absent, that the earlier unconditioned association was causally generated by taxon turnover alone, or that selection had no effect.

Analogous sample audits (recorded shared status-genus × island units with differing floral symmetry):
- Tropical continental **4/241**, with only 9 physical islands satisfying at least two matched original genera.
- Northern-midlatitude oceanic **0/21**, northern-midlatitude continental **0/93**.
- These units may reuse genera/accepted names across islands and are NOT phylogenetically independent experiments. They do show strong recorded categorical floral symmetry conservatism among the actually matchable taxa.

## Stronger research interpretation: two separable steps

**1. Colonization and plant assemblage formation.** Potential GIFT source pool, botanical genus composition and recorded floral functional-diversity differences can be assessed observationally under original geological labels. This does NOT observe actual arrivals, established propagules or past extinction. The stringent oceanic/continental matched geography yields only three region-disjoint island pairs per major region.

**2. Post-establishment trait divergence and functional adaptation.** Historical adaptation needs same-lineage or island/mainland population phenotypic differences, independent native/endemism qualification, a credible true phylogenetic partner and measured pollinator/pollen-limitation/seed-set context. Direct categorical symmetry data from status-matched congeners are nearly invariant in this source; phenotypically labile floral colour, size, nectar-guide, mating system and direct local photo measurements are better future data targets, pending actual source opportunity and trait variability gates.

**Scientific ceiling:** retain prior macro floral source-dependent assemblage observations, but do NOT promote either the −0.0156 unconditioned symmetry association or the exactly zero same-genus result to a confirmed ecological or evolutionary mechanism. Keep manuscript and source PR DRAFT.
