## Source-congener expected discordance: original actual categorical states, not a prediction model

For an observed strict endemic or strict native-nonendemic accepted plant species with a Direct high/medium single-category trait state, retrieve independently observed OTHER accepted species of the **same genus** from original source GIFT donor flora that also have original species-Direct trait states; exclude ALL plant names recorded anywhere on the focal island from candidate source lists.

Outcome for each island×plant, for one trait: fraction of original source congener species whose **actual recorded categorical state** differs from that focal species. This is an EXACT expected mismatch under uniform one-source-congener draw; it is NOT the nearest *morphologically favorable* donor, nor an ancestral state reconstruction or a fitness outcome. Multiple source congeners enter with their actual trait-state frequencies.

For each physical island and trait, require >=3 endemic + >=3 native_nonendemic distinct focal species with original source congeners before calculating the first island-level status difference. Whole 10-degree physical spatial-block and first-nearest GIFT source signature cluster-equal conditional bootstrap, 1,999 resamples, only if >=10 islands, >=5 blocks and >=5 first source signatures. A second **same-island matched botanical genus** control requires >=2 distinct *identical* genus groups represented by BOTH original endemic and native-nonendemic Direct species, sharing the same GIFT donor genus reference. Its result is a distinct selected cohort/estimand; do NOT treat raw and matched results as a causal mediation fraction.

**Primary trait hypotheses** before donor mismatch inspection: floral colour and floral symmetry. Other 8 trait effects are exploratory acquisition/identifiability endpoints. No 10-trait post-hoc p-value discovery is promoted.

## Main support-limited result: tropical oceanic symmetry

- **15 tropical oceanic physical islands / 13 spatial blocks / 11 nearest GIFT source signatures** support the island-level comparison: source-congener **endemic − native-nonendemic symmetry mismatch = −0.01558099**, physical-block CI in two numeric seeds approximately **[−0.0236,−0.0085]**; source-signature mean **−0.01536876**, source CI approximately **[−0.0242,−0.0074]**. Original endemic island×taxon source-congener symmetry observations in the complete origin/region cohort: **189**, not 189 independent paired-island status contrasts. The negative value indicates endemic source-congener states are descriptively MORE similar to GIFT source congener categories than native-nonendemic comparison taxa, not an 'adaptive simplification'.
- **Same-genus control**: require same island, same botanical genus appearing among both native-nonendemic and endemic original source-congener-comparable species. Only **12 tropical oceanic islands / 9 spatial blocks / 7 candidate source signatures** support >=2 identical shared genus groups. In this selected cohort the species-direct symmetry status contrast is **exactly 0.0000** under both geographic and source weighting, with bootstrap interval **[0,0]** for both numeric seeds. This means observed categorical states were identical within the selected matched original genera; it does **not** demonstrate whole-island/lineage evolutionary equivalence or absence of unmeasured phenotype differences.
- Other main region×origin groups have **fewer than 10** qualifying both-status islands for the initial comparison (tropical continental 9, northern midlat continental 8/oceanic 6). In both tropical oceanic and continental cohorts, colour has too few same-island both-status pairs (7 versus 9). **Zero origin contrasts across all 10 traits** meet the frozen bilateral oceanic AND continental minimum (10 islands / 5 blocks / 5 GIFT source signatures each). Consequently **NO geological-origin-specific endemic divergence coefficient is identified**.
- Source original phenotypes are one category per accepted plant species globally. Genetic variation, within-species colonization trajectory, pollinator exposure and reproductive fitness are NOT observed. The exact zero matched-genus result is compatible with the previously documented strong original genus conservation of symmetry, and cannot identify its evolutionary cause.

## Scientific conclusion and next gate

**The apparently endemic-associated tropical oceanic symmetry difference does not survive comparing *different endemic/nonendemic species inside the same recorded genus*.** Current evidence emphasizes botanical genus composition and data provenance rather than independently repeated evolution of a single island pollination syndrome. The result after same-genus control is an important falsification of any simple 'endemic => novel symmetry' argument, but this is a selected within-12-island matched comparison and no formal difference-in-estimands or source/provider-independent causal decomposition.

For a genuine establishment-vs-adaptation analysis, prioritize the specific evidence gap:

1. Collect oceanic AND continental, confidently indigenous plant populations with mainland conspecific/sister comparisons, trait measurements in the SAME local population (colour variation, size, tubular depth, reproductive systems and nectar guides), not just a species-wide static trait category.
2. Estimate within-lineage transitions on independent tree tips/branches (TPL/LCVP/WP are backdrop/source sensitivity; exact ancestor not provided by the nearest donor genus), accounting for phylogenetic covariance, genus conditioning, source abundance, area, isolation and geological archipelago dependence.
3. Couple trait changes with actual flower visitors, pollen deposition or seed set to test whether pollinator scarcity or replacement mediates the difference. Separate potential colonizer supply (GIFT candidate inventory) from true ecological establishment probability.
4. Revisit *floral colour and reproductive assurance traits* with independently recorded within-genus variation, since current symmetry is nearly invariant in the strict comparable same-genus panel.

All source PRs remain **DRAFT**. This exploratory ecological study does not establish adaptive evolution, oceanic island source filtering, real visitor shift or pollination syndrome effectiveness.
