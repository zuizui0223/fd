## Scientific question and prior flaw

An earlier apparently directional global association for the original GBIF Bombus guild and plant **flower colour × floral form** was calculated on 760 GBIF-detected islands versus 23 adequately surveyed/source-aware GBIF nondetections, while the original nearest-five GIFT candidate-mainland source-adjusted result used only 50 vs16 OTHER islands. It is invalid to attribute the smaller source-adjusted coefficient solely to controlling donor trait states without first fixing the SAME island cohort.

The new analysis freezes every source/accepted plant/geography/guild record and outputs, for each of 13 Direct trait/trait-pair endpoints, both original GIFT-native and all-recorded botanical status scopes, three independently catalogued GBIF guild states, globally and by region:

1. Raw finite n=5 categorical Rao on the original source-available GBIF guild detected/qualified-nondetected floras.
2. Raw Rao on EXACTLY the same island names and qualifying plant traits used for source-relative Rao.
3. Original GIFT candidate-mainland source-relative Rao on that SAME island cohort.
4. Physical 10° geographic block and independent GIFT first-nearest-source group effects measured **WITHIN clusters that contain both guild observation statuses**. Original previous separate-cluster block-equal differences do NOT ensure the same spatial block was observed in both states.
5. Actual directly documented floral trait fraction in each same-source-group cohort. This is plant/literature documentation, NOT real island colour prevalence.

No source island, plant, GBIF species, observer, photo, or original GIFT region IDs are exported.

**Interpretation:** source-comparable same-island cohort selection alone changes the point estimate substantially. Referencing the native GIFT candidate source shrinks it further on the same original cohort. However these are comparisons between DIFFERENT geographic blocks, not randomized or phylogenetically matched guild exposures. They do not demonstrate that actual Bombus observation/nonobservation drives island plant flower architecture.

Among the 50/16 source-comparable ORIGINAL islands, Bombus detected/nondetected groups occur across **22 versus 8** physical blocks and **19 versus 6** separate candidate GIFT first-source signatures. **ZERO** physical blocks contain BOTH statuses; **ZERO** first-source signatures contain BOTH statuses. Thus **0/8** fixed *exploratory post hoc* guild/flower-source diagnostic slots can be evaluated within five genuinely mixed spatial/source blocks; all eight are reported as UNSUPPORTED, not p=0 and not evidence of no real biological effect.

Further source documentation bias on the exact same 50/16 islands:

- Flower colour + floral form jointly Direct-documented plant fraction: **0.144668 versus 0.100878**, a +4.38 percentage point difference in botanical sampling/documentation.
- Floral form Direct-documented fraction: **0.355182 versus 0.251510**, a +10.37 percentage point difference.
- Differential source documentation can co-vary with geography and recorded botanical composition, so do NOT repackage this difference as true pollinator-associated floral function.

## Original RAW source populations also change when comparing WITHIN shared clusters

For the full raw original 760/23 Bombus vs colour×form source-available island sample:

- **Only 4 physical 10° blocks** have any islands from BOTH GBIF states, BELOW the frozen >=5 independent block gate; withhold this contrast.
- At the independently first-ranked GIFT candidate-source level **6 groups** contain BOTH states; corresponding *within-physical-source-group* raw joint Rao difference **−0.0025672**, exactly enumerated 2^6 = 64 two-sided descriptive cluster-sign diagnostic **p=0.78125**. Block-bootstrap 95% interval from the same six group differences **[−0.010624,+0.003627]** under seed 20261114 and approximately [−0.010636,+0.003795] under seed 20261115. Does NOT demonstrate a guild/selection effect.
- Raw colour×form pairing departure on 6 mixed GIFT source signatures: mean **+0.0015464**, cluster-sign p=0.71875; interval includes zero.
- These analyses keep all 4,505 original floras in the source denominator; the four/six mixed blocks are a diagnostic about whether a narrowly defined guild-state *contrast* is identifiable, NOT the main biodiversity population.

Independent example: **tropical bird-recorded versus effort-qualified bird-nondetected island floral symmetry** has original source n=593/13 botanical floras. Original unadjusted separately-block-weighted contrast had positive intervals; after requiring both animal statuses IN the same geography, **10 physical 10° mixed blocks** have descriptive mean **+0.045904**, CI about **[−0.000522,+0.092895]**, exact cluster sign p=**0.083984375**. At eight genuinely mixed first-GIFT-source groups, descriptive mean **+0.0325692**, CI about **[−0.011745,+0.098862]**, sign p=**0.4375**. Neither is robust at a nominal 0.05 level, nor does a bird GBIF record prove avian flower visitation or pollen deposition.

## Manuscript position

**Retain the entire 4,505-island botanical flora and all separately directly recorded floral/reproductive functional traits as the primary global ecological assemblage analysis.** Pollinator-channel associations are explicitly source/effort/geographically confounded observational evidence, not original ecological selection estimates. No geological origin modulation is yet estimable from this GBIF qualified nondetection source; 425 original geologically labelled botanical floras and sparse nondetections do not create independent geographic contrasts.

True visitor changes, pollination effectiveness, colony presence, ancestry, selection, adaptive trait plasticity, or actual historical guild loss require independent biological evidence not supplied by these GBIF detections or GIFT species-wide Direct categories.

A separate source-effort audit is implemented to use the preserved original NEE guild **background_record_count, background_spatial_units, background_temporal_units and distinct_dataset_count** alongside the original 4,505-island botanical trait documentation fractions; its results should be added after independent source CI completion.
