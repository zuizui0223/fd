A second, independent original-source join [island Actions #43589819228](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/actions/runs/43589819228) SUCCESS, anonymous artifact #14111529368, counts the five-channel guild status combinations and exactly corresponding true single-/pair-floral DIRECT source-plant sample opportunities on the **full 4,505** botanical island cohort, rather than selecting 928 complete profiles.

## Scientific position and concrete next falsification

The broadly meaningful ecological result is that island floral trait repertoires and local animal guild recording systems CAN be joined on all 4,505 documented original floras without a 3-trait full-case restriction. **However, adequate source-aware guild nondetection is rare**, and phenotype/GBIF recording effort differs by geography and botanical/animal composition. Globally significant-looking raw morphological patterns therefore cannot yet be promoted to causal pollination syndromes or actual island adaptation.

Keep as primary global macro: 4,505 island flora original single-trait and two-trait functional diversity, source-relative genus equalization, region, native-status and limited geology context. Keep GBIF animal status-guild associations as an observation-process-aware, explicitly correlative layer. Same-plant recorded visitors, actual pollen delivered, reproductive assurance, autonomous seed set and bona fide historical changes are REQUIRED to infer actual pollinator shifts/selection.
