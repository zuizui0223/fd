# fd manuscript claim / identification audit — 2026-11-17

**Status: analysis of already verified original-source aggregates, not a new original-data test. Exploratory; keep fd PR #16 DRAFT.** Scope remains the **4,505** accepted GIFT botanical island floras. The historical 928-triple-complete and 17D Gaussian-KDE volume are supplemental only. This memo adds a deliberately conservative cross-region contrast and situates the actual observations against prior island-colonization and trait-evolution literature; it does not invent effective-pollinator observations.

## Main biological question

**When island floras turn over taxonomically, do their documented floral functions collapse, recur, or reassemble into different regional combinations, and how much of the source-relative pattern accompanies botanical lineage assembly rather than distinct congeneric trait states?**

Use *animal-pollinated flower morphology hypotheses*, not labels that assert actual Bombus, bird, butterfly or Sphingidae pollination. Floral architecture indicates potentially accessible flower types; verified effective visitor identity requires separate data.

## Original verified results, nested evidence levels

1. **Functional overlap is real but not proof of convergence.** Among original 2,324 floras with >=5 high/medium Direct flower colour×form characterizations **on the same species**, 2,699,326 full-flora unordered island pairs have mean TV-frequency overlap **0.386900734** and full-flora taxonomic Sørensen-Dice overlap **0.061305358** (different indices: their numerical gap is NOT a standardized ecological effect). Restricting to the 842,058 pairs with no species in common yields mean floral category overlap **0.247183275**. This describes recurring finite categories under major species replacement, **not** within-species evolution. In those disjoint pairs observed mean squared frequency distance is **0.2297** against a particular same-mask within-genus-label-exchange expected **0.2078**, descriptive excess **+0.0218**. Thus the observed positive overlap does **not** establish unusually strong functional convergence relative to the genus null. See [source-verified full global beta report](../results/20261115_global_island_functional_beta/README.md) and [actual main figure audit](../results/20261117_global_4505_main_figures/README.md).
2. **Biogeographically conditional source-relative configuration frequencies.** Same accepted-plant Direct **actinomorphic × deep tube** share minus geographically nearest five potential native mainland GIFT candidate sources: tropical **+10.989 pp** (666 eligible floras; nominal 95% physical-block interval [+8.958,+13.313]), northern high latitude **−2.704 pp** (135; [−3.562,−1.895]); northern midlatitude **+2.580 pp** (1,443), southern extratropical **+4.103 pp** (118). The descriptive tropical-minus-high-latitude gap is **+13.693 pp**. Combining the separately reported marginal block-interval endpoints gives a **conservative endpoint range [+10.853,+16.875] pp**: if each regional nominal interval really achieved >=95% marginal coverage, the simultaneous coverage of this constructed gap range is >=90% by the union bound even without cross-region independence. This is **not** a newly bootstrapped 95% contrast or a fresh multiple-test-adjusted discovery. Source classes and distinct island/plant masks matter.
3. **Exact within-scope source-genus signed identity** for that tropical actinomorphic×deep response is

   `+10.989 pp = +7.309 pp` (shared-genus weights) `+0.706 pp` (states of different accepted source and island species within shared genera) `+11.847 pp` (island-exclusive documented genera) `−8.873 pp` (candidate mainland-only recorded genera).

   The three botanical genus-presence/weight terms have signed net **+10.283 pp**, and the within-shared-genus distinct-species term **+0.706 pp**; these are **not percentages of a causal mechanism**, because positive and negative parts cancel and donor/recording bias is nonrandom. Exact sum verified in the original [four-term paired report](../results/20261117_global_4505_main_figures/README.md); distinct marginal single-axis partitions must not be substituted.
4. **Independent phenotype measurement helps taxonomy but not a visitor mechanism.** NHM numerical corolla length source-congener models with genus-heldout ordinal calibration predict island Direct deep-tube labels better than the same numerical donor species pooled. Tropical all-recorded geo5 gain **+0.10803 Brier** (425 islands), and after removing the five most represented documented focal genera **+0.05605** (different selected 274 islands); source-corpus holdouts BHL, China, North America and Pakistan remain positive. However strict known-native n68 block interval for geo5 **includes zero** and source/island documentation-balanced cases are only **8/425** for the broad tropical panel. This establishes selected morphological predictability, not independent island changes in centimeters or documented pollination. Sources: [numeric trait audit](../results/20261116_nhm_numeric_tube_source/README.md), [provider holdout](../results/20261116_nhm_numeric_provider_disjoint/README.md), [genus concentration](../results/20261116_nhm_numeric_genus_concentration/README.md).

In the original GloBI exact island×plant visitor-citation screen, after omitting the single globally dominant archive-first source, the named bird and undivided Lepidoptera documentary counts are **zero for all three true deep-tube morphological categories**. This is **absence of resilient documentation**, not ecological absence of birds or Lepidoptera. Web of Life and Mangal offer additional real island network edges but no independent effective bird/butterfly/sphingid evidence for this manuscript claim. ## Novelty boundary against pertinent literature

- **Hetherington-Rauth & Johnson (2020), Am. Nat. doi:10.1086/709018**: 556 species and 136 phylogenetically independent island-mainland sister comparisons; no universal floral-size island syndrome and ecological filtering explicitly proposed as an alternative. Therefore *“island floral traits are context dependent”* alone is NOT original here.
- **Zell et al. (2025; published online 2024), New Phytol. doi:10.1111/nph.20234**: 3,222 flowering-plant species; island colonization, SC, floral symmetry, life history, arrival opportunity and taxonomy jointly analyzed. Therefore *“trait-related colonization / lineage filtering”* alone is NOT novel.
- **Barajas Barbosa et al. (2023), Nature doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06305-z**: Tenerife native-flora functional space already partitions colonization and in-situ diversification. Do not claim the first island trait-space / assembly analysis.
- **Ollerton et al. (2009), Ann. Bot. doi:10.1093/aob/mcp031; Rosas-Guerrero et al. (2014), Ecol. Lett. doi:10.1111/ele.12224**: pollination syndromes can be tested with independently observed animal visitors and pollination effectiveness; phenotype alone is not visitor identification. Island bird-pollination systems can be generalist and mismatched with textbook syndromes (see Functional Ecology doi:10.1111/1365-2435.70068 and doi:10.1111/1365-2435.14493).

**Defensible potential contribution here:** an unusually broad global island-flora frame, true same-species direct flower-category pairs without triple-completion, quantitative taxonomic-versus-functional reassembly, *signed exact mainland candidate genus accounting*, and transparent testing of the limits of ecological guild attribution. This remains botanical macroecology rather than verified pollinator-driven adaptation.

## Independent visitor-guild data update: actual co-visitation is not a syndrome switch (17 November 2026)

Since this manuscript audit was first drafted, original source-protected [DoPI × GIFT study/site/year/month positive multiguild co-visitation](../results/20261117_original_dopi_gift_guild_motifs/POSITIVE_MULTI_GUILD_COACCESS.md) has completed successfully ([original run #44098890581](https://github.com/zuizui0223/island/actions/runs/44098890581)). On directly characterized botanical plant names from the full 4,505 GIFT-island population, SAME accepted plants can carry positive visitor citations for both Bombus and Syrphidae (or butterfly/Bombus) in a single published European site and period. This establishes recorded multiple-guild visitation opportunity, **not** proven simultaneous flower visits, negative guild observations, pollen effectiveness or island adaptation. The raw Bombus–Syrphidae open radial morphology contrast is **−6.84 percentage points** [−11.35,−1.96] relative to the union of *positively* documented plants, not positive as an overgeneralized 'open flowers always co-visited' prediction would suggest. Source-genus-fixed morphological nulls are supportable in **only 3/135** same-site/year/month guild×flower profile panels and **0/135** pollen-carriage grade1/2 panels. Their individual conditional intervals are post hoc and unadjusted. No floristic parent island denominator is narrowed, and neither the causal guild arrow nor historical bird/butterfly/hawkmoth transition is identified.

Earlier [DoPI study-equal and genus-equal tests](../results/20261117_original_dopi_gift_guild_motifs/GENUS_EQUAL_WITHIN_STUDY_AUDIT.md) and [source-locality temporal tests](../results/20261117_original_dopi_gift_guild_motifs/SAME_PUBLISHED_SITE_TIME_VALIDATION.md) likewise found broad visitor–morphology architecture associations but strong plant-lineage and observation bias. These are explicitly **external European positive visitor compatibility** strata, not individual original island pollinator community surveys or causal evidence of flower evolution. Relevant independent oceanic island network study: [Xia et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7434763/) documents more real visitors than classic syndrome expectation, again separating visitors from most effective pollinators.

## Most discriminating preregistrable next comparison (not executed here)

Keep the full 4,505-island population as the roster, then let each floral sector retain its own exact eligible same-plant mask and **same island set** when comparing models. The principal response is `D_i = p_island_i(actinomorphic×deep) − p_candidate_source_i(actinomorphic×deep)`, and a separately named source-conditioned botanical reference `M_i` is obtained *without using the focal island's trait outcomes* from source-only different accepted plant names, documented botanical genera and source composition. Test **out-of-source, out-of-archipelago prediction** of `D_i` or residual `D_i−M_i` using strictly native plant records and independent phenotypic sources, compared with (a) region/climate/area/distance/annotation effort; (b) genus/source-opportunity; (c) true verified guild availability and quantitative effective visitation. Archive and physical blocks, plant genus identity and botanical provider must be grouped during cross-validation. **Do not use the existing GloBI/GBIF nondetection as biological guild loss**, and do not fit a supposedly identified guild coefficient on the currently sparse matched contrast.

A genuine pollinator-mechanism claim requires a prospective, effort-comparable local plant×visitor observation set with effective pollen transfer, ideally geographically and taxonomically independent. A historical postcolonization adaptation claim additionally requires real population floral traits in island plants versus conspecific or phylogenetically justified mainland relatives and evidence separating ancestry, dispersal, persistence and fitness. Geologically **oceanic versus continental** comparisons are a nested historical design, **425/4,505** typed island floras, not a default proxy for actual guild absence.
