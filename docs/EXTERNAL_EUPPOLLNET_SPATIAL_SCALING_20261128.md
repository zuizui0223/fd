# Geographic scaling and crossed dependence audit (2026-11-28)

External-only, post-discovery continuation of FD PR84. The positive fixed-margin conditional channel residual +.044582 is supported across 14 studies, but repeated plant taxa across studies may induce additional dependence and the geographic-scale relationship is unknown. No new own-data sources, labels, rosters or coordinates. All focal coordinates are from the original public EuPPollNet v1.3.0.

Replay parent 3043 pairs and +.03952796255; replay fixed-margin 2537 retained pairs and +.04458227814 before any new estimate. Pin both source blobs and original null settings; do not reselect pairs. Normalize exact two-token plant names with existing publisher parser, exclude collisions. Genus from exact first token, not a phylogenetic tree.

Two distinct analyses:
1. Robustness of known level (not new primary hypothesis): average fixed-margin residual over site pairs within plant-study-year, equal plants within year, years within study, studies overall. Independently resample studies and genera with multinomial weights; product-weight (pigeonhole) bootstrap on fixed original cell weights, 9999 draws. Also study-only and genus-only and leave-one-study/genus. Require >=10 studies, >=50 species, >=20 genera; positive crossed interval and both deletion minima to retain positive level under this stricter dependence audit.
2. New geographic-scale hypothesis: excess channel concordance increases with distance if compositional replacement across more separated local pools preserves channels beyond fixed local degree and overlap. For each plant-study-year, estimate slope of pair residual on log2 great-circle separation (per doubling), requiring >=3 distinct positive distances spanning at least twofold. Equal eligible plant cells within years, years within studies, studies overall, same crossed-bootstrap and support gates. Report observed and null slope components on exactly matched cells; do not interpret coarsening alone as a biological result. A positive crossed interval and both deletion minima are required for the directional hypothesis. A nonpositive or unresolved slope does not prove invariance or absence of geography. Both-exclusive-sets >=2 descriptive sensitivity, no promotion if primary fails. This is an exploratory conditional inference, not calibrated spatial independence or a global island effect. No independent new sites; no causal functional compensation or actual effectiveness. Site-pair dependence within studies, species overlap and taxonomic structure limit generalization. No threshold changes after seeing outputs.
