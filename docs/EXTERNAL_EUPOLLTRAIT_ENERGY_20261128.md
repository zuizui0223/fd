# Size distribution conservation versus within-set dispersion (2026-11-28)

External-only post-discovery extension of FD PR84. The supported original +.110649 mean cross-exclusive absolute log2 ITD distance reduction does not uniquely identify similarity of trait distributions: cross distance contains both within-set spread and between-distribution divergence. The family-adjusted gain is largely compatible with visitor-genus trait distributions (permutation p=.506). Do not rescue a genus residual by changing taxa or thresholds. Instead freeze the different, biologically necessary estimand of distribution similarity on exactly the original 1261 matched bee comparisons.

Let U,V be the two exclusive visitor sets of a focal plant. Define C=mean_{x in U,y in V}|log2ITD_x-log2ITD_y|; W=(mean_{x,x' in U}|x-x'|+mean_{y,y' in V}|y-y'|)/2, using all ordered pairs including zero self distances (empirical distributions). Then H=C-W is half the one-dimensional energy distance, equivalently integrated squared difference of empirical CDFs. H>=0 and is zero for identical distributions. For observed and fixed-degree/overlap conditional null, C=W+H. Hence gain C_null-C_obs = (W_null-W_obs)+(H_null-H_obs). Positive distribution gain means closer size distributions than expected, positive dispersion gain means reduced average within-set spread. These components are arithmetic, not causal contributions. Unlike cross distance alone, H separates between-distribution difference from within-set size heterogeneity.
