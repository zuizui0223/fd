# External-only cross-site visitor replacement (2026-11-28)

Separate estimand, not a rescue of the nonestimable matched island–continent contrast in PR84. Use only pinned original EuPPollNet v1.3.0 records and its original metadata. No protected flora, trait ledger, island labels, or selected own-data cohort. No historical loss, temporal rewiring or pollination effectiveness claim.

Question: when the same plant species has different observed visitor species at two locations, are the exclusive visitor taxa more likely to belong to the same broad channel than expected from the locally observed visitor pools? Use six mutually exclusive classes: bees (seven named families), other Hymenoptera, Diptera, Lepidoptera, Coleoptera, other orders. Family concordance is a descriptive taxonomic sensitivity, not an independently observed functional trait.

## Frozen analysis before outcome
- Same study and year, metadata explicitly naming the four insect orders, valid positive species-level visits, order/family resolved. Merge all records at identical coordinates within study-year; use all unique plant–visitor edges, not visit abundance. Exclude conflicting visitor taxonomic labels within study-year. At least five plant and five visitor species per site.
- For each same plant at two distinct sites, let A and B be the sets of observed visitor species, U=A minus B and V=B minus A. Condition on both exclusive sets being nonempty. Observed concordance = fraction of all cross-pairs U × V with matching channel. Report conditional cohort attrition and taxonomic Jaccard.
- Each site's background comprises visitor species observed on OTHER plants at that site, weighted by number of those distinct plant partners. Exact null = sum over different species x,y of local degree weights times indicator of matching channel, divided by sum of weights over different species pairs. This subtracts the same-species terms from both numerator and denominator. Require at least two background taxa each side. This is a local observed-availability null, not a degree-preserving whole-network null; it does not control floral abundance, phenology, detection or unmeasured visitors. Uniform background weighting and at least two exclusive visitor species each side are sensitivities. No substitution of an easier endpoint if primary fails.
- Equal site-pair weights within plant × study-year; then equal plants, equal years within study, equal studies. Bootstrap 9,999 draws over studies (fixed seed), leave-one-study-out. Primary support gate: >=10 studies, >=50 distinct focal species, >=30 distinct study-year site pairs. Positive residual and both lower 95% bootstrap bound and minimum leave-study mean >0. Study-level conditional inference does not remove all repeated-species or shared-source dependence. Family and uniform null results are descriptive; no multiplicity-based promotion.
- New question motivated by failure of island–continent matched support, but no channel-retention outcomes inspected before freezing. It is not an island-specific test or independent dataset replication. If positive, it establishes nonrandom coarse visitor-channel concordance across sites relative to the specified observed background; it does not establish equivalent pollination or compensation. If support fails, keep null/limitations and seek independent sources, not threshold changes.
