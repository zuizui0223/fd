# Original true-tip botanical phylogeny × native floral pairing: inference contract

**2026-11-14 analytical extension of original fd PR #16; post-hoc to the previously observed 928-island Rao / colour–architecture results. The source outcome is recorded botanical flora, NOT pollinator shift or pollen transfer.**

## Prior result and unresolved causal rival

[Previous source-backed 928-island taxon-identity-matched pairing](../results/20261114_native_provenance/NATIVE_TAXON_MATCHED_PAIRING_928.md) found positive *all-recorded tropical* source-relative n30 colour–architecture pairing departure (+0.00651, 106 islands), but no supported independent positive tropical residual under same-genus / same-CoL-candidate-family n5/n8 donor matching. One n8 northern midlatitude native family-matched different-genus residual was negative, explicitly post-hoc.

We now stress that result under three independent **published dated-tree exact-tip mappings** (TPL, LCVP, WP, but with mostly shared megatree provenance), not inserted taxonomic tips. A plant must have strict Direct colour/form/symmetry, a CoL XR exact candidate family consistent with original GBIF-reported family, and a genuine source backbone tip. Available original exact-tip species are TPL 1,187; LCVP 1,162; WP 1,160 of original Direct 2,234 source plants. These trees and plant source floras were verified before the current analysis. Do not treat branches as newly sequenced independent populations.

## Original sampling / source comparators

- Analysis unit: **one original island**, with 24 distinct fixed-seed samples per island used only to average the same island record; 24 draws are NOT independent biological outcomes.
- Cohort: exactly **928** originally eligible island floras, three nested origin tiers (all-recorded, WCVP TDWG-L3 *regionally* native-compatible, strict original GIFT native). Retain original >=30 complete Direct species gate *before* true-tip filtering, and report the additional chance to match five distinct published source tips as a support result.
- Candidate mainland: original five geographically nearest GIFT native-region source checklists, NOT confirmed ancestors. Remove **every source-Direct plant name present on the focal island** from all donors, regardless of provenance tier.
- Original CoL family map remains *unreviewed automatically source-consistent candidate taxonomy*, not curated phylogenetic reconstruction.
- Matching never sees flower colours or visitor outcomes. Original true-tree path thresholds are 50 and 100 **published branch-length units**, chosen for sensitivity, not biological dispersal times. Strict one-to-one bipartite island–source different-accepted-species matching within the same candidate family. A third same-genus within-family and d<=100 condition is a finer taxonomic stress.
- Main metric at n=5: \`D_pairing = (4/5) * (S_color * S_architecture - S_joint)\` where S is distinct-original-species same-profile probability. Report **island-minus-GIFT donor** departure and same focal island/plant score against an *unbounded distance, same family* donor control. Marginal and joint Rao Q are separate secondary endpoints. This is a signed quadratic coupling, NOT mutual information, network pollination efficiency or evolutionary selection.
- Same exactly five focal plants re-matched to independently paired **near (distance <=50)** and **mid (50 < distance <=100)** donor species, each same candidate family, when both distinct-source-donor networks can be matched; this is a stronger source-selection countercomparison but remains observational.
- Support gates: ≥10 separate physical islands, ≥5 physical geographic 10-degree blocks and ≥5 first-nearest GIFT source signatures => **exploratory conditional regional interval**. ≥20 islands, ≥10 blocks and ≥10 first-source signatures => **stronger sampling support**, not independent evolutionary replication. Unsupported groups show N only; never print a pseudo-zero, impute missing visitors or rescue significance by lowering thresholds.
- Original-tree TPL and WP are **backbone sensitivities** for primary LCVP interpretation, not independent evolutionary replications. Two original execution seeds likewise check numerical stability only.
- Entire original botanical taxon, tree-tip identity, GIFT source entity, island polygon and CoL assignment stays inside rights-restricted CI. Only anonymous region/origin/condition/clustering aggregate JSON is public.

## Interpretation rules (fixed before the new three-tree numerical outcomes)

1. A positive **full-flora n30** tropical contrast does not equal a positive **n5 true-tip matched** result; changes in the target population and per-flora trait repertoire are explicit.
2. An interval crossing zero on matched source taxonomy, tree branch or WCVP-native strata is **not a demonstration of equivalent flora** and does not show the full-flora positive effect was entirely due to family identity.
3. If exact published close-tip source matching is unreportable in native tropical islands, say **unidentified due to source/trait/phylogenetic opportunity** instead of extending the all-recorded model into its missing taxa.
4. For supported effects, require consistency in sign under both whole physical-block and nearest GIFT-source-signature cluster weighting before calling a directional *descriptive* pattern.
5. **No** finding from species-fixed flower colour/form/symmetry data identifies *within-species* floral phenotype change or historical bee/bird/moth pollinator shifts; original static GBIF guild states do not supply 20/20 supported detection contrast and GloBI citation composition does not supply effective pollen transfer.
6. The ultimate mechanistic test needs independently surveyed local pollinator visits, pollen transfer or seed set, and indigenous plant origin data with reasonably independent islands/lineages. The source current study is macroecological floral assembly.
