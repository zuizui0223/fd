## New target
Does excess plant disconnection under hypothetical removal of all observed bee species differ between island and continent **for the same plant species in the same study and year**? This compares actual observed links with equal-sized random visitor species removal, not morphology proxies or historical losses. EuPPollNet v1.3.0 publisher README reports 623,476 rows, 54 studies and 1,864 networks; actual qualifying support is not known yet. Source DOI 10.1111/geb.70000; publisher distribution https://github.com/joseblanuza/EuPPollNet/tree/v1.3.0/Data/3_Final_data. Verify original compressed interaction file SHA256 plus Git blob, and verify metadata against its exact publisher Git blob with computed SHA256 archived. Only metadata explicitly naming Hymenoptera, Diptera, Lepidoptera and Coleoptera in the same study-year is eligible (documented taxonomic coverage, not necessarily protocol completeness). Positive visit records, species ranks, resolved family/order and georeferenced network-site-year keys; remove contradictory family-to-bee labels within network. All observed visitor species including nonbee families are eligible, without imputing unrecorded taxa.

For plant degree d, network visitor richness n and number of observed bees m, expected disconnection under removal of m uniformly selected species is C(m,d)/C(n,d) (zero when d>m). Observed disconnection is 1 if all observed partners are bees. Use their difference as conditional vulnerability, retaining zero/all-bee networks when >=5 plants and >=5 visitors. This null conditions on observed richness and plant degree, not phenology, traits, phylogeny, detection or network degree of removed pollinators. No causal inference or fitness claim.

Use newly retrieved public GSHHG 2.3.7 high-resolution L1 polygon membership (pinned archive hash). Island: individual landmass area 20 to 7,000,000 km²; continent: >7,000,000 km². Exclude unresolved, boundary, ambiguous and small landmass sites; coordinate precision limits remain. Use site-network-year equal weights within each exact shared plant, then plant-year equal within study, and study equal overall. The geographic comparison is conditional on matched species and studies, not representative of all European islands. Support requires >=8 matched studies, >=20 matched species, >=5 island landmasses and >=5 occupied 5-degree blocks on each side. Positive or negative block-unadjusted study bootstrap 95% interval and same-sign leave-study-out range required; residual inference remains exploratory because study and species overlap can induce dependence. If support fails, report descriptive estimates and do not relax gates. No raw names or coordinates exported. Absolute observed and null components, eligible-network counts, and geographic support accompany the primary endpoint; no additional guild shopping.
