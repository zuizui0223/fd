# Breeding-system calibration: novelty and data gate

## Completed external GIFT branch

The official GIFT 3.1 expansion (fd PR #72, run 45573747562) is not a supported island-specific reproductive-assurance result. Of 288 selected island entities and 75 mainland comparators, 78 pairs pass reference-pattern cell support. The geographic-block contrast is +0.00719 (95% conditional bootstrap interval -0.02517 to +0.03680); first-comparator weighting gives -0.00384. Added targets alone give -0.01453. Genus-weight sensitivity and all five reference leave-outs fail to establish a directional effect. Five strictly flagged native pairs are insufficient. Do not select a positive subgroup or expand the geography further to hunt significance.

## Prior work and scope

Razanajatovo et al. (2016), DOI 10.1038/ncomms13313, reports quantitative self-compatibility (SC) and autofertility (AF) indices for 1,752 species. The same compilation has already been used to test global island colonization in Razanajatovo et al. (2019), Global Ecology and Biogeography 28:341–352, DOI 10.1111/geb.12854. That study found modest support for selfing ability among island colonists, not higher selfing ability among island endemics. A repeat of the 2019 colonization regression is not a new research result.

Ferrer et al., DOI 10.1093/aob/mcae056, reports mating-system information for 5,685 taxa, including SI and partial SC. This is a distinct compilation, not automatically independent experiments.

The proposed next test is **measurement calibration**, not another test of the marginal island association. Are publisher-coded GIFT self-fertilization states associated with measured AF after conditioning on measured SC, and does concordance differ between coded insect and wind pollination? A true ecological guild mechanism would still require verified pollinator observations.

## Analysis gate

Exact accepted binomial matching first; audit duplicate species, synonym resolution separately, experiment and study overlap. Preserve fruit-based and seed-based SC and AF separately. No genus-level imputation, no conversion of SC to AF. First report matched species, studies, genera and insect/wind cell support. Within-study/lineage contrasts are exploratory unless there is adequate overlap. Source-level GIFT code provenance remains necessary. Prefer the calibration of AF conditional on SC over marginal differences. All null and non-estimable outcomes retained; no island-effect confirmation from this calibration alone. Do not fit until source bytes and data dictionary are verified. No own-project database inputs.
